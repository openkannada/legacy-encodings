Short excerpts of third-party documents

The files marked with this identifier hold short excerpts of documents by others,
mostly works of the Government of Karnataka. The sources carry no license statement,
and the licenses of this repository do not cover the excerpted text.

They are kept, each line with the URL of its source, for research on and testing of
text converters. No document is stored in full. Keeping short excerpts with their
sources for research and testing is meant as fair dealing under the Indian Copyright
Act, 1957.

To ask for an excerpt to be removed, write to support@luminik.io or open an issue in
the repository.
